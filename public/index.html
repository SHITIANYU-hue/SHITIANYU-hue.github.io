I am an AI Research Lead with a Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Toronto. My research focuses on reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and large language model agents, with applications in intelligent transportation, decentralized AI, and generative modeling. I collaborate globally with academia and industry to advance trustworthy and scalable AI. If you are interested in collaboration with me, feel free to send my email below.
