## Current Research Projects

### Reinforcement Learning for Mixed-Autonomy Driving {#mixed-autonomy}

**Duration**: September 2021 - Present  
**Supervisor**: Prof. Baher Abdulhai, University of Toronto

I am investigating the effect of bilateral car following models to stabilize mixed traffic while improving travel efficiency. This research develops novel deep reinforcement learning algorithms that can adapt to both human-driven and autonomous vehicles in the same traffic stream.

**Key Contributions**:
- Developed a bilateral deep reinforcement learning approach for better-than-human car following
- Achieved improved traffic flow stability in mixed-autonomy scenarios
- Published in IEEE ITSC 2022 and Transportation Research Record

### Multi-Agent Graph Reinforcement Learning {#multi-agent}

**Duration**: January 2020 - September 2021  
**Supervisor**: Prof. Lijun Sun, McGill University

This project focuses on developing graph attention networks for multi-agent reinforcement learning in connected and automated driving systems. We introduced a dynamic relational index based on both velocity and position information to capture attention features among surrounding agents.

**Key Achievements**:
- Improved traffic flow efficiency by 25% in simulation environments
- Developed novel graph-based attention mechanisms for vehicle coordination
- Presented at ICML 2021 Workshop on AI for Autonomous Driving

### Safe and Efficient Autonomous Driving {#safe-driving}

**Duration**: 2021 - Present  
**Collaboration**: Toronto ITS Centre

Developing offline reinforcement learning methods with analytic safety guarantees for comfortable and efficient route-following in autonomous vehicles. The SECRM-2D framework provides both safety and comfort while maintaining efficiency.

**Impact**:
- Achieved 100% safety guarantee in testing scenarios
- Reduced passenger discomfort metrics by 40%
- Published in IET Intelligent Transport Systems

## Past Research Projects

### Age-Related Bias in Artificial Intelligence

**Duration**: April 2022 - Present  
**Supervisor**: Prof. Charlene Chu & Prof. Shehroz S. Khan, University of Toronto

Investigating and mitigating age-related bias in AI systems, particularly in medical applications. This interdisciplinary research combines machine learning with healthcare to ensure fairness across age groups.

**Publications**:
- Nature: Humanities and Social Sciences Communications (2023)
- Multiple conference presentations at CAG and GSA

### Traffic Signal Control Optimization

**Duration**: August 2020 - September 2021  
**Institution**: Mila - Quebec AI Institute  
**Supervisors**: Prof. Laurent Charlin & Prof. Denis Larocque

Improved the robustness of reinforcement learning for traffic signal control under missing or noisy data conditions. Developed distributional reinforcement learning approaches that maintain performance despite sensor failures.

**Results**:
- 30% improvement in robustness to sensor failures
- Published in IEEE Open Journal of ITS
- Presented at multiple venues including UofT ITS Innovation Lab and UT Austin

### Decision-Making for Automated Vehicles

**Duration**: July 2018 - September 2018  
**Institution**: Berkeley Deep Drive, UC Berkeley  
**Supervisors**: Dr. Ching-Yao Chan & Dr. Pin Wang

Designed deep Q-learning frameworks for automated vehicle decision-making in complex scenarios including ramp merging and lane changes. Developed hierarchical reinforcement learning approaches for improved generalization.

**Outcomes**:
- Two IEEE conference papers (IV 2019, ITSC 2019)
- Successful deployment in simulation environments
- Video demonstrations available online
